Mira Fragrance is a UAE-based online store selling tester perfumes.

What is a tester?

A tester is a fragrance supplied for sampling in a store. It is the same kind of bottle as the retail version, but it comes in a plain tester box instead of the full retail packaging, and it is usually priced lower. All perfumes we sell are testers, supplied in their original box.
