This page explains what personal information Mira Fragrance collects and how we use it.

What we collect

When you order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address and email so we can deliver your order and contact you about it. Our online store platform, Shopify, processes this information for us.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order.
  • To reply to your messages, including on WhatsApp.

Sharing

We share your details only with the people needed to complete your order, such as our delivery partner. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and analytics

Our store uses cookies to keep your cart working.

Your choices
